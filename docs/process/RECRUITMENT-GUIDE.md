## 2. A Much Faster Route: Official Hack2skill Discord

Searching the dashboard search bar without knowing whether the person is active or already has a team often wastes time. The best route:

1. **Join the Official Hack2skill Discord:**
   The official Hack2skill community link is accessible directly from the `aibuildercup.com` portal or the Hack2skill server.
2. **Open the `#team-formation` or `#find-a-team` Channel:**
   In this channel, participants post full bios, including:
   - Country of origin (there is a flag role).
   - Tech stack (Frontend / Backend / AI / UI-UX).
   - Status: *Looking for team (LFT)*.
3. **Direct Message Template (Professional English):**
   ```text
   Hi [Name],
   I saw your profile in the AI Builder Cup Discord. Our team is building Assay (Track 4: Media, Content & Digital Experiences) — an active Socratic learning engine designed to fight AI Brain Rot with Honeypot cheat-detection.

   We already have the architecture and PRD locked, and we're looking for one dedicated team member to collaborate with us for the Singapore Grand Finale. 

   Let me know if you're still looking for a team and interested in checking our project blueprint!
   ```

## 3. Organizer Regulation Warnings (Compliance Check)

* **Maximum Team Size:** 4 people (minimum 2). If your team currently has 3 people, you can add only **1 more person**.
* **Professional Eligibility:** Make sure the candidate is at least 21 years old and not an active student (a hard requirement of the AI Builder Cup).
* **Confirm Before Inviting:** Don't fire the *invite button* on the dashboard without chatting first; if the person doesn't click accept on their dashboard, your team slot can be left hanging right before the 18 October deadline.
